# Engineering onboarding

Welcome to the platform group. This page lists what to set up in your first week.

## Access

- Request access to the engineering database through the access portal. Read-only access is
  granted automatically; write access needs approval from the owning team.
- Join the chat channel of your team and of every team whose services you will deploy.

## Local development

Every service builds with a single command from its repository root and runs its tests the same
way in CI. Use the shared container images for databases instead of installing them locally.

## Your first deployment

Pair with someone from your team on your first production deployment. Follow the
[deployment runbook](runbooks/deploying.md) and watch the dashboards for at least fifteen minutes
afterwards.
